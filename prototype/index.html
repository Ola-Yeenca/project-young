House of Young started as a collective putting on nights for the African diaspora in Valencia. It grew into an entertainment company with a second home in Lagos. We produce events, represent and book artists, and keep a record of every room we fill.

How do I buy tickets?

Every event has a Buy tickets button. It opens our ticket partner for that city, where you pay and get a QR ticket by email.

How do I book an artist?

Open the artist and send an enquiry with date, location and audience. We reply within one working day with availability and a quote.

Can we collaborate on an event?

Yes. Pick Collaboration below and tell us the venue, date and what you bring.

Do you work outside Valencia and Lagos?

We travel for the right booking. Send the location and we will confirm.

Press and media

Pick Press below and we send the press kit.

Goes to the HOY inbox and Admin → Enquiries. You get a confirmation email.
